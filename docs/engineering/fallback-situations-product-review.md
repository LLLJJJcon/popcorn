## 1. 强调程度 · 朋友家人闲聊

你和朋友爬山，平时半小时的路今天走了两个小时。朋友问：“今天这段路和上次比怎么样？”

**对话对象**：朋友
**题目要求**：Reply to your friend and emphasize how unusually demanding the route was.

**英文对照**：You and a friend are hiking. A route that usually takes half an hour took two hours today. Your friend asks, “How does today's route compare with last time?”

## 2. 强调程度 · 工作与学校

**对话对象**：同事
**题目要求**：Reply to your coworker and emphasize how unusual this week's workload is.

**英文对照**：You have meetings every day this week and even eat lunch at your desk. A coworker asks, “Is this week's workload the same as before?”

## 3. 表达不满 · 朋友家人闲聊

## 4. 表达不满 · 日常办事
